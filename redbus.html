Coimbatore to Chennai Bus Overview

The Coimbatore to Chennai bus route covers approximately 508 kms and takes about 9 hrs 7 mins. A total of 218 direct buses operated by 90 bus operators run on this route. The first bus departs at 00:10, and the last bus leaves at 23:59. 

Major boarding points in Coimbatore include Aathupalam, Annur, Anthiyur, Athipalayam Privu, Avinashi Road, Bharathi Nagar, Bharathiyar University, Chinniyampalayam, Chithode, Eachanari, while common drop locations in Chennai are Adyar, Alandur, Ambattur, Aminjikarai, Anna Nagar, Anna University, Arumbakkam, Ashok Pillar, Avadi, Central Railway Station.

RTC and private operators such as IntrCity SmartBus, SRL Travels, YBM Travels(BLM), JB Connect, City Travels offer Volvo, AC, and non-AC seater and sleeper buses. Ticket prices on redBus range from INR 400 - INR 6000.00 depending on the bus type and features.
